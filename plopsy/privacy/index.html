Plopsy — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Plopsy is an Android game where you drop little animals into a box and merge matching ones into bigger ones. This policy explains what the game does with information. In short: the game has no account system of its own and no server of its own. Your progress stays on your phone, and the only information that leaves it goes to Google services you use through the game.

1. Information that stays on your phone

The game stores the following only in its private storage on your phone. None of it is sent to us, because we run no server:

  • your best score, the animals you have unlocked, your helper items, and a game you paused and have not finished;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, reduced motion);
  • whether you bought Remove Ads.

This data is excluded from Android cloud backup. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all of it.

2. Google Play Games

The game signs you in to Google Play Games Services, using the Google account on your phone, so that your scores can appear on leaderboards and your achievements can be unlocked. When you are signed in, the game sends your scores and achievement progress to Google. Google shows your player name and avatar on leaderboards according to your Play Games settings, and handles that information under Google's Privacy Policy. You can sign out in the game's Settings or manage your Play Games profile in the Play Games app. The game can still be played without signing in, with your best score kept on your phone.

3. Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; the game never sees your card or payment details. The game only learns whether Google Play reports that you own Remove Ads, and keeps that answer on your phone. Restoring a purchase uses your Google account on Google Play.

4. Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob, such as ads between games and ads you choose to watch to continue a game or to earn a helper item. When ads are shown, Google and its ad partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to serve and measure ads. Depending on your region and your choices, ads may be personalised or limited. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy, which appears whenever your region requires it.
  • If you buy Remove Ads, the ads shown between games are removed; the purchase screen says exactly what remains.
  • You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings.

5. Statistics and crash reports

The game contains no analytics software and no crash-reporting software of its own. Google Play may give us aggregated statistics, such as the number of installs and crashes, collected by Google from devices whose users chose to share usage and diagnostic data with Google. We cannot identify individual players from them.

6. What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or profiles of our own.
  • No tracking of what you do in the game, and no analytics software.
  • No sale of personal information, and no data sent to a Plopsy server, because there is none.

7. Children

Plopsy is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices and rights

Because the game keeps its data on your phone, you control it directly: you can clear all game data at any time in Android settings. For information handled by Google (Play Games, ads, Google Play), use the controls in your Google account and Android settings. If you have a question or a request about this policy, write to us at the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches players.
