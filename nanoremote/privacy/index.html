Nano Remote — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

Nano Remote is an Android app that turns your phone into a remote control for your Android TV or Google TV. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: the app has no account system and no server of its own, and what you do with your TV stays between your phone and your TV.

1. Information that stays on your phone

The app stores the following only in its private storage on your phone. None of it is sent to us, because we run no server:

  • the TVs you have paired (name and local network address);
  • the security certificate and key the app generated to pair with your TV, kept in app storage and excluded from Android cloud backup, so that a restored backup cannot act as your phone;
  • your macros, the buttons you pinned to the remote, your language choice, and whether you own Nano Remote Pro.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all of this.

2. Local network access

The app asks for network permissions so it can find your TV on your Wi-Fi and talk to it directly, over an encrypted connection (TLS), using the Android TV Remote protocol. Discovery and control traffic goes only between your phone and your TV on your local network.

3. Microphone

The microphone is used only for voice search, and only while you hold or tap the microphone button. The audio is streamed to your TV over your local network. The app does not record it to storage and does not send it to any server of ours. What your TV does with the audio is governed by the TV and its voice assistant provider, not by Nano Remote.

4. Text you type for the TV

Text you type into the app's keyboard field is sent to your TV over your local network so that it appears in the TV's text field. The app does not keep it.

5. Purchases

Nano Remote Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; the app never sees your card or payment details. The app only learns whether Google Play reports that you own Pro, and keeps that answer on your phone. Restoring a purchase uses your Google account on Google Play.

6. Advertising

The free version shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google and its ad partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to serve and measure ads. Depending on your region and your choices, ads may be personalised or limited. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • If you use Nano Remote Pro, the app shows no ads and does not start the advertising SDK.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options, which appears whenever your region requires it.
  • You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings.

7. What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or user profiles.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service at the time of this policy. If one is added, this policy will be updated before the app ships with it.
  • No sale of personal information, and no data sent to a Nano Remote server, because there is none.

8. Children

Nano Remote is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your choices and rights

Because the app keeps its data on your phone, you control it directly: you can remove a paired TV, delete macros, or clear all app data at any time. For information handled by Google (ads, Google Play), use the controls in your Google account and Android settings. If you have a question or a request about this policy, write to us at the address below.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches users.
